How It Works

Three steps to
sovereign ownership

No accounts. No cloud. No trust required — the device handles everything on-chip.

01

Device arrives blank

Your Blockshiel ships completely factory-fresh with no pre-loaded keys. You generate your 24-word seed phrase directly on-device during first setup — it never leaves the chip, not even for a millisecond.

02

Connect your app

Install the companion app on your phone or desktop. Pair via USB-C or Bluetooth. Your wallet becomes accessible — but keys stay on the hardware at all times.

03

Approve physically

Every single transaction must be verified and confirmed using the two physical buttons on the device. The amount and recipient appear on-screen. Nothing moves without your press.
